Horse rehoming

Need Help With an Icelandic Horse?

Life changes, and sometimes a horse’s needs grow beyond what one owner or barn can provide. Reaching out is a caring choice. We’ll listen without judgment and help you find the best path for your horse.

Start a rehoming request

What to Expect

  1. Tell us about the horse

    Share as much as you can using the form below: health, history, training, temperament, and your timeline.

  2. We talk it through

    We’ll follow up to understand your situation. Sometimes resources or a connection in the community can help a horse stay home.

  3. Plan the transition

    If the horse needs a new place to land, we work with you on intake, foster placement, or a direct match with an adopter.

  4. Stay connected

    We’re happy to share updates on how your horse is doing in their next chapter.

Is a horse in immediate danger?

If a horse is injured, being neglected, or at risk of going to auction, contact your local animal control or law enforcement right away, then let us know. Our capacity is still growing, so we can’t promise immediate intake, but we will always do our best to help or connect you with someone who can.
