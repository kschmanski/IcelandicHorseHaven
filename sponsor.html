How to help

Sponsor a Horse

Not able to adopt? A sponsorship lets you support a specific horse’s care month after month, and follow their story along the way.

Sponsorship Levels

$25 / month

Friend

Helps keep a horse in good hay and fresh water.

$50 / month

Guardian

Helps cover regular trims and hoof care.

$100 / month

Herd Hero

Helps cover vaccines, dental work, and fly protection for sweet itch season.

$250 / month

Haven Champion

Helps cover a horse’s full monthly care.

Every sponsor receives updates and photos of their horse.

Horses You Can Sponsor

Our sanctuary residents are horses who will stay with us for life because of age, health, or special care needs.
