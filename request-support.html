Need Help With an Icelandic Horse?

Use this form to request help for an Icelandic horse in the United States. Tell us what is needed and how we may be able to help, whether that’s veterinary or hoof care, feed, boarding, transport, foster care, or finding the horse a new home.

Start a support request

What to Expect

  1. Tell us what’s needed

    Share the horse’s situation and the kind of help you’re asking for using the form below.

  2. We review and follow up

    We’ll be in touch to talk it through, and we may ask for more information, photos, estimates, or records.

  3. Agree on a plan

    Any approved support, payment arrangements, or placement terms are confirmed with you separately.

  4. Stay connected

    We’re happy to keep in touch and hear how the horse is doing.

Is a horse in immediate danger?

This form is not an emergency response service. For an urgent medical concern, contact a veterinarian directly. If a horse is injured, being neglected, or at risk of going to auction, contact your local animal control or law enforcement right away, then let us know.
