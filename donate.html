How to help

Your Gift Could Change a Horse’s Life

Donations provide hay, hoof care, veterinary treatment, and safe transport for Icelandic horses who need somewhere to land.

What your gift can do

  • $25 helps keep a horse in good hay
  • $50 helps pay for a hoof trim
  • $100 helps fund a fly sheet for a horse with sweet itch
  • $250 helps cover a veterinary exam or dental float
  • $500 helps bring a horse in need to safety

Supplies & in-kind gifts

Fly sheets and masks, hay, hoof boots, and tack in Icelandic sizes are always welcome. Contact us before dropping anything off.

Tribute gifts

Honor or remember a person or a beloved horse with a gift in their name. Just tell us who it’s for.
